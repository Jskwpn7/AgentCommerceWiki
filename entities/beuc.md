# BEUC — The European Consumer Organisation

## Overview

The European Consumer Organisation (BEUC; Bureau Européen des Unions de Consommateurs) is the largest organisation promoting the general interests of Europe's consumers. Founded in 1962, it represents more than 40 independent national consumer organisations from over 30 European countries.

## Key Dates and Facts

- **Founded:** 1962
- **HQ:** Rue d'Arlon 80, B-1040 Brussels, Belgium
- **Membership:** 40+ national consumer organisations from 30+ countries
- **EC transparency register ID:** 9505781573-45
- **Contact:** digital@beuc.eu
- **Website:** www.beuc.eu

BEUC informs EU policies to improve people's lives in a sustainable and fair economy and society. It publishes formal consultation responses, position papers, and open letters on EU digital legislation and consumer protection.

## Documented Harms or Failures

BEUC itself is a consumer advocacy body, not a regulated entity. However, it has documented and campaigned against numerous consumer harms in areas including:
