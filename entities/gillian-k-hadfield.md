# Gillian K. Hadfield

## Overview

Gillian K. Hadfield is a legal scholar and economist known for her work on institutional design, complex systems, and the intersection of law and technology. She has written extensively on how legal and regulatory institutions adapt (or fail to adapt) to technological change.

## Key Contributions to AI and Markets

She co-authored [[hadfield-koh-2025-economy-of-ai-agents]] with Andrew Koh, a foundational economic analysis of how AI agents may reshape markets, organizations, and institutions.

Her work consistently emphasizes:
- The importance of institutional design in managing technological change
- The inscrutability of modern AI systems as a regulatory challenge
- The need for legal frameworks that can handle non-human actors
- The gap between technical capability and our understanding of its implications

## Publications

- [[hadfield-koh-2025-economy-of-ai-agents]] — Economic implications of autonomous AI agents (2025)
- Multiple papers on institutional design, complex systems, and regulation

## Relevance to UK Consumer Harms Research

Hadfield's work on institutional design for AI agents is directly relevant to UK consumer protection frameworks. Her arguments about the inadequacy of existing legal infrastructure for non-human actors, the opacity of foundation models, and the need for adaptive regulation provide a theoretical foundation for understanding the gaps in current UK consumer law.
