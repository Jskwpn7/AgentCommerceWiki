# Agentic AI Commerce: The Next Wave of Online Shopping and Retailer Risk

Sheppard Mullin | July 2026

## Content

Legal analysis identifying risks to retailers from third-party AI shopping agents accessing their sites.

## Key Legal Issues

**Agent Authorization:** One major ecommerce site obtained a preliminary injunction to prohibit agents from transacting on the site. Court found that despite user giving agent permission, the agent accessed user's password-protected account **without authorization of the site operator**. This creates a fundamental legal question: does consumer consent to their agent override retailer terms of service?

**Risk Categories:**
1. New fraud and scams via AI agents
2. Liability when agent makes unauthorized purchase
3. Enforceability of website terms of service against agentic transactions (user may never have seen/agreed to terms)
4. Privacy issues when site shares user information with third-party agent
5. Agent authentication — can retailers identify and authenticate legitimate agents?

**Industry Response:** NIST launched AI Agent Standards Initiative through CAISI to support development of interoperable and secure AI agent systems. Consumer Bankers Association met fall 2025 to scope consumer protection issues with agentic payment tools. Various entities developing protocols for agentic commerce transactions, agent-to-agent communication, product discoverability by agents, and agentic payments.

## Retailer Recommendations
- Assess updates to ecommerce site technology (identify/authenticate agents, ensure authorization)
- Update terms of service to address AI agent issues
- Consider privacy implications of sharing user information with third-party agents
