# AI Shopping Assistant Guide 2026: Agentic Commerce Protocols

Opascope | By Max Thilén | 33 min read | Last updated April 16, 2026

## Content Summary

Comprehensive guide covering the two competing agentic commerce protocols (ACP and UCP), their technical specs, costs, and implications for ecommerce brands. Key finding: **customers are already shopping without ever visiting websites** — AI agents search feeds, surface competitor products with Buy buttons, and customers check out inside the conversation.

## The Format War: ACP vs UCP

| Protocol | Owner | Status | Where It Lives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ACP** (Agentic Commerce Protocol) | OpenAI + Stripe | Live since Sept 2025 | ChatGPT (900M+ WAU, US only) |
| **UCP** (Universal Commerce Protocol) | Google + Coalition | Announced Jan 2026 | Google Search AI Mode, Gemini (20+ partners: Walmart, Target, Shopify) |

This is described as a format war where both will coexist — brands need to support both, "the same way they run Google Ads and Meta Ads in parallel." ChatGPT excels at conversational discovery; Google AI Mode captures high-intent search queries.

## Market Projections
- McKinsey: $900B-$1T US retail revenue from agentic commerce by 2030, $3-$5T globally
- Morgan Stanley/AlphaWise: LLM adoption approaching 50% in US; AI agents capture 10-20% of e-commerce ($190-$385B)
- Forrester: 20% of B2B sellers will face agent-led quote negotiations by end of 2026

## ACP (Agentic Commerce Protocol) Details
- Built by OpenAI + Stripe, launched September 29, 2025
- License: Apache 2.0 (open source)
- Spec: agenticcommerce.dev
- How purchase works: User asks ChatGPT → AI searches merchant feeds → Products appear with personalized recommendations and Buy button

## UCP (Universal Commerce Protocol) Details
- Announced January 2026 at NRF, backed by Google + coalition partners
- Partners include Walmart, Target, Shopify, and 20+ other major retailers
- Integrated into Google Search AI Mode and Gemini

## Brand Challenge
"Is your product data ready for AI agents to find and sell? What happens to your brand if it isn't?" Entire shopping funnel can happen in a conversation with zero brand visibility — no click-through, no landing page.
