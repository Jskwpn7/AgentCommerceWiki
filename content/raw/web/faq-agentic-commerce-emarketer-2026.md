# FAQ on agentic commerce: How brands should act now to compete in an AI-driven landscape

EMarketer | By EMarketer Editors, Arielle Feger | 7 April 2026

## Content

Agentic commerce — where AI agents autonomously discover, compare, and purchase products on behalf of consumers — is emerging as one of the defining commerce trends of 2026. Amazon, Google, OpenAI, and Meta have all launched AI shopping tools in the past year.

**Definition:** Agentic commerce is online shopping in which AI agents autonomously discover, compare, and purchase products on behalf of consumers. Unlike chatbots that respond to individual queries, agentic systems combine memory (recalling user preferences), tool access (connecting to retailer databases and payments), and multi-step reasoning to handle transactions end-to-end. The concept spans Amazon's Rufus, OpenAI's ChatGPT, and Perplexity's agentic shopping tool.

**Key players shaping the landscape:**
- **Amazon:** Rufus includes "Auto Buy" capabilities authorizing purchases when items hit target prices
- **OpenAI:** ChatGPT enables consumers to complete purchases via third-party apps plugged into the chatbot
- **Google:** AI Mode uses Google Shopping Graph's 50 billion-plus product listings for conversational product discovery
- **Google UCP:** Universal Commerce Protocol backed by 20+ global partners signals industry-wide push toward standardized AI shopping infrastructure

Brands and advertisers must prepare for a landscape where AI agents act as intermediaries between consumers and retailers, potentially bypassing traditional marketing channels.
