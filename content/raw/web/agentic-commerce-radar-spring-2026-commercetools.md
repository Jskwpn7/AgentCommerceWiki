# The Agentic Commerce Radar: Spring 2026 Update

commercetools Blog, published 22 April 2026

## Content Summary

**Key takeaways:**
- From AI-assisted shopping to the reality of stalled autonomous checkout
- 63% of European shoppers use AI for comparing options; 51% would be open to full AI handling including final purchase
- Gap between 65% trusting AI for comparison vs. only 14% trusting it to place orders — the critical consumer trust bottleneck
- OpenAI paused Instant Checkout; autonomous shopping may take longer than anticipated
- In short term: brands must make product catalogs, inventory, payments and trust signals machine-readable
- Long term: machine-readable data becomes entry-level to compete in agent-driven market

## Consumer Sentiment Data (Spring 2026)

- **63% European shoppers** use AI for comparing options like brands, models, prices and reviews
- **55%** use AI to learn about a category or product
- **53%** comfortable letting AI manage recurring purchases automatically
- **51%** of shoppers would be open to AI handling entire shopping process including final purchase
- **62% Australian consumers** open to AI agents helping with purchasing decisions (everyday categories)
- **65% US consumers** trust AI to compare prices; only **14%** trust it to place orders on their behalf
- Trust highest among Gen Z (29%) and millennials (30%)
- **79%** say accuracy is most important factor in AI-powered shopping

## Protocol Developments Mentioned

- Google pushing open protocols such as UCP
- Sephora developing own AI path
- OpenAI's Instant Checkout paused due to performance issues

## Key Signal

The gap between 65% trusting AI for comparison vs. 14% for purchasing is the most important signal: consumers trust AI to inform decisions but not yet to make them. Adoption will be gradual, driven by repeated positive experiences. Data accuracy and reliability are prerequisites for autonomous commerce adoption.
