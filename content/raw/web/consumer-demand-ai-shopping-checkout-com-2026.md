# Consumer demand for AI shopping is forming fast but trust for agentic commerce is still catching up

Checkout.com | 9 June 2026

## Content

**Research basis:** Checkout.com research report "Agentic Commerce 2026: The State of Consumer Demand and Merchant Readiness"

Key findings:
- **33% of consumers** expect at least 10% of purchases to be AI-driven within a year
- **72%** of merchants agree consumers will adopt agent-led shopping faster than most merchants are prepared for
- **24%** of consumers say they will never delegate purchases to AI
- **27%** trust no organization to operate an AI shopping agent
- Not just an expectation gap, but a **trust gap**

### Consumer Controls Required
On average, consumers would allow an AI shopping agent to spend £177 per purchase without additional approvals (across 6 markets surveyed) — lower than merchant assumption of £200.

Top non-negotiables for feeling confident with agent-led shopping:
- Spending caps (30%)
- Instant revocation (29%)
- Easy cancellation (28%)

### Motivation Drivers
- **25%** say saving time is top motivator
- **20%** want to use AI to ensure they never miss a better deal

### Category Adoption Expectations
Most willing to delegate: groceries (41%), household supplies (31%) — low-risk, repeatable categories.
Least delegable: financial services (15%) — high consideration purchases. Merchants expect agentic commerce to take hold first in complex decisions like financial products, creating a mismatch.

### Brand Loyalty Impact
**57%** of consumers would let an AI shopping agent switch brands if it found better value — fundamentally challenging brand loyalty models.

### Current State
According to merchants across UK and US, only **3% of transactions involve AI agents**, but **89% of merchants are actively preparing** for agentic commerce.

Quote from Rory O'Neill (CMO): "Until those foundations [security, refunds, permissions, spend limits] are in place, trust will remain one of the biggest barriers to adoption."
