# Agentic Commerce: What Brands Need to Know About AI Agents Shopping on Their Behalf

Charle | Published May 2026

## Content Summary

Brand-focused analysis of agentic commerce from a UK-based brand representation platform. Covers how AI agents are changing brand discovery, comparison and purchase workflows — and what it means for brands that don't optimize their product data for machine consumption.

Key themes include the shift from human-to-product to agent-to-product interactions, the importance of structured product data (feeds, APIs), and how brands risk invisibility if they fail to make their commerce information agent-readable.

Mentions protocols including Google's UCP and OpenAI/Stripe's ACP as emerging standards that determine which brands AI agents can discover and recommend.
