# Shopping in the Age of AI: Redefining Stores for a New Era

**Source:** McKinsey & Company / ICSC  
**Authors:** Colleen Baum (Senior Partner, McKinsey), with ICSC collaboration  
**Published:** April 27, 2026  
**Format:** Report + webinar (July 13)  
**URL:** https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/retail/our-insights/shopping-in-the-age-of-ai-redefining-stores-for-a-new-era

> **Note:** Original McKinsey page behind paywall; content derived from ICSC press release and industry coverage. Report was in McKinsey's Top 10 Reports — Second Quarter 2026 list.

## Methodology

Based on interviews with retail and real estate leaders and a consumer survey of **3,004 US consumers**. Covers grocery, fashion, health and beauty, home, electronics, and food and beverage sectors.

## Three Forces Reshaping Retail

1. **Growing use of AI in the shopping journey** — AI-mediated search and purchase automation transforming how consumers discover, compare, and buy
2. **Rising expectations for transparency and convenience** — consumers demand faster, clearer, more reliable shopping experiences
3. **Shifts in consumer spending power and behaviors** — evolving demographics and purchasing patterns altering retail dynamics

## Key Finding: Convenience vs. Discovery — The New Shopping Calculus

As AI agents handle more routine shopping, retailers must clearly deliver one of two distinct value propositions:

### Convenience-Optimized Stores
- Emphasize speed, intuitive navigation, in-stock reliability
- **37% of consumers** cite convenience as a top driver of store visits
- AI-mediated search handles discovery upstream — stores become fulfillment points
- Lower friction, transactional focus

### Discovery-Led Formats
- Prioritize engagement through experiential retail, digital storytelling, personalized service
- **40%+ of Gen Z and millennials** say experiential retail makes them more likely to shop a retailer
- Stores as destinations for exploration and experience
- Higher touch, relationship-building focus

## Economic Stakes

McKinsey estimates the **top decile** of retailers executing this strategy will capture **more than 85% of sector economic profit**. The gap between winners and laggards is widening rapidly.

## Agentic Commerce Forecast

- McKinsey projects up to **$1 trillion in U.S. B2C retail revenue** from agentic commerce by 2030
- AI agents increasingly handling routine shopping tasks autonomously
- Store visits may become less frequent but more purposeful
- Physical stores not eliminated — the bar for what they deliver is raised

## Consumer AI Usage (Pre-Purchase)

- **62%** of shoppers use AI to compare brands, models, prices or reviews
- **54%** use AI to learn about a category/product (feature prioritization)
- Nearly **50%** turn to AI for discovery and inspiration
- **68%** of consumers used at least one AI-enabled tool in the past three months

## Strategic Recommendations for Retailers

1. **Define each store's mission clearly** — every location must have a specific strategic purpose (convenience OR discovery, not both)
2. **Align layout, assortment, technology, and talent** to deliver on that chosen role
3. **Use technology as an enabler** — reduce friction in convenience-led stores; enhance service/personalization in discovery-led environments
4. **Curate tenant mixes** — blend retail, dining, and services into cohesive destinations (for shopping center operators)
5. **Operational redesign + disciplined capital allocation** required to execute

## Key Quotes

> "This report presents an opportunity for retailers and property owners: A clear mission for their physical footprint is essential to stand out in a landscape of intelligent technologies and attract consumers to their stores and centers."  
> — **Tom McGee**, President and CEO, ICSC

> "AI isn't eliminating the store—it's raising the bar for what it needs to deliver. As more of the shopping journey moves upstream, the stores that win will be those with a clearly defined role—whether enabling speed and certainty or creating experiences worth the trip."  
> — **Colleen Baum**, Senior Partner, McKinsey & Company
