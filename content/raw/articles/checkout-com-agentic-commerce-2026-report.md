# Checkout.com "Agentic Commerce 2026: The State of Consumer Demand and Merchant Readiness" Report

**Source:** Checkout.com (global digital payments company)
**Date:** June 9, 2026
**URL:** https://www.checkout.com/newsroom/consumer-demand-for-ai-shopping-is-forming-fast-but-trust-for-agentic-commerce-is-still-catching-up
**Full Report:** https://www.checkout.com/guides-and-reports/agentic-commerce-2026

## Overview

A consumer survey of 12,000+ consumers and Heads of Payments across six countries (UK, US, UAE, and others) examining consumer demand for AI-driven shopping and merchant readiness for agentic commerce.

## Key Statistics

| Statistic | Value | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Consumers expecting 10%+ of purchases AI-driven within a year | **33%** | Demand is forming rapidly |
| Merchants agreeing consumers will adopt faster than businesses are ready | **72%** | Preparedness gap is wide |
| Consumers who will never delegate purchases to AI | **24%** | Trust deficit is significant |
| Consumers who trust no organization with an AI shopping agent | **27%** | Major trust barrier |
| Merchants already testing agentic commerce | **42%** | Industry adoption accelerating |

## Key Findings

### Consumer Demand vs. Trust Gap

The report identifies a **growing gap** between emerging consumer demand and the trust, control, and infrastructure needed to support agentic commerce. Consumers believe in the potential of AI to transform shopping, but organizations must build trust through:
- Clear controls
- Permissioning mechanisms
- Ease of cancellation

### Consumer Trust Requirements

Consumers will only delegate spending to an AI agent if **strict controls exist**:
- Spending limits must be settable
- Categories of purchase must be configurable
- Cancellation must be easy and immediate
- Transparency about what the agent is doing

## Connection to UK Consumer Harms

| Vector | Description |
|---|---|
| **Trust deficit** | 27% trust no organization with an AI shopping agent — the largest trust barrier identified across all sources |
| **Informed consent** | Consumers demand clear controls and permissions — but current agent interfaces do not provide these |
| **Consumer Rights Act 2015** | Cancellation rights must be meaningful and accessible for agentic commerce |
| **DMCCA 2024** | If merchants cannot demonstrate trust controls, they may breach "fair trading" obligations |
| **Market structure** | 42% of merchants already testing agentic commerce suggests this is not a future risk but an active deployment |
| **Payment infrastructure** | As a payments company, Checkout.com's perspective reflects the technical infrastructure layer — the rails that enable or constrain agentic commerce |

## Comparison with Other Sources

- **Human Security** — published a nine-step adoption framework covering trust stack and RAG bot visibility
- **nShift** — emphasized delivery and returns data as the structured information AI agents need to compare offers
