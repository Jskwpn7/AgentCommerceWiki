# Algorithmic Shopping Is Here. Is Your Company Ready?

> **Author:** Paul F. Accornero, Harvard Business Review
> **Publication:** HBR, 7 August 2026 (AI and machine learning topic)
> **URL:** https://hbr.org/2026/08/algorithmic-shopping-is-here-is-your-company-ready
> **Type:** Executive analysis — "AI as the customer"
> **Access note:** HBR is paywalled; the article summary and opening paragraph were extracted on 2026-08-17. The remainder of the body is summarised from the monitor report's detailed entry, which worked from the same article.

## Summary (verbatim from HBR)

Executives have spent years preparing their organizations to use AI internally. The next challenge is preparing for AI as the customer. As intelligent shopping agents increasingly evaluate, [select, and purchase on behalf of consumers, merchants must redesign storefronts, product data, and commercial strategy around machine-readable, agent-optimised surfaces.] *(bracketed completion per monitor report; original paywalled)*

## Opening paragraph (verbatim from HBR)

In April 2025, Amazon launched "Buy for Me," which lets its AI agent visit brand websites, select products, enter payment details, and complete purchases without the customer ever leaving the Amazon app. By September, OpenAI had introduced "Instant Checkout" with the open-source Agentic Commerce Protocol, enabling purchases directly inside ChatGPT. In January 2026, Google unveiled the Universal Commerce Protocol at the National Retail Federation conference: an open standard, built with Shopify, Target, Walmart, and more than 20 other partners, covering the shopping process from discovery to post-purchase support. Microsoft launched Copilot Checkout at the same conference, and Shopify has since switched on agent-readable storefronts by default across millions of merchants. Gartner now projects that by 2028, 90% of B2B purchases, more than $15 trillion, will flow through AI agent exchanges.

## Key content (per monitor report detailed entry, B.2)

- **Timeline consolidation:** Amazon Buy for Me → ChatGPT Instant Checkout (ACP) → UCP (NRF, Jan 2026) → Copilot Checkout → Shopify agent-readable storefronts default-on.
- **"AI as the customer" framing:** the customer of record is increasingly an agent; merchant design (AIO / agent-intent optimization) becomes the new gatekeeping layer.
- **Gartner projection:** 90% of B2B purchases ($15T+) via AI agents by 2028.
- **Harms relevance (report analysis):** AIO/agent-intent optimization shifts manipulation risk from human-facing dark patterns to machine-facing ranking/presentation; consumers lose the ability to see the alternative surface their agent saw.
