# Veterinary Services Market Consumer Harms

**Status:** CMA investigation launched
**CMA consultation:** Draft substantive orders and undertakings (3 August 2026)

## Overview

Pet owners face significant consumer harms in the veterinary services market, including information asymmetry, high switching costs, and emergency pricing. The CMA's market investigation represents a critical intervention in this essential services sector.

## Key Consumer Harms

1. **Information asymmetry:** Pet owners cannot easily assess veterinary service quality or pricing
2. **High switching costs:** Emergency situations limit switching opportunities
3. **Emergency pricing:** Lack of transparency during urgent care situations
4. **Pricing opacity:** Difficulty comparing prices between practices
5. **Quality information gaps:** Limited accessible quality metrics

## CMA Investigation

The CMA is consulting on draft substantive orders and undertakings to address these harms. The investigation examines:

- Pricing transparency requirements
- Switching facilitation measures
- Quality information disclosure
- Market structure effects

## Regulatory Response

- **CMA consultation** (3 August 2026): Draft substantive orders and undertakings

## Consumer Protection Implications

- Enhanced pricing transparency requirements
- Improved switching mechanisms
- Quality information standardisation
- Emergency pricing oversight
