# "Stop-the-Clock" — Delaying AI Act Implementation

## Definition

The "Stop-the-Clock" approach is a Commission proposal to introduce a conditional mechanism where key high-risk AI obligations apply only after the Commission confirms that supporting compliance tools (harmonised standards) are available. This creates a "pause" in the AI Act's implementation timeline.

## How it Manifests in the UK

UK regulators could face similar pressure from industry to delay compliance deadlines, undermining consumer protections and creating regulatory fragmentation between UK and EU markets.

## Examples and Evidence

- **Conditional mechanism:** High-risk obligations apply only after Commission confirms harmonised standards availability [[raw/papers/BEUC-X-2026-007-Digital-Omnibus-AI.pdf]]
- **Postponed deadlines:** Annex III systems delayed to 2 December 2027; Annex I systems delayed to 2 August 2028 (if no Commission decision is adopted) [[raw/papers/BEUC-X-2026-007-Digital-Omnibus-AI.pdf]]
- **Labeling requirements delayed:** Transparency and labelling requirements under Article 50 are also postponed — consumers cannot exercise rights without knowing AI systems are being used [[raw/papers/BEUC-X-2026-007-Digital-Omnibus-AI.pdf]]
- **Industry-delayed standards:** Civil society and standardisation organisations have "consistently denounced that the industry actors calling for a 'stop the clock' are the same who are mainly responsible for delaying" standardisation [[raw/papers/BEUC-X-2026-007-Digital-Omnibus-AI.pdf]]
- **Contested from inception:** BEUC, civil society, lawmakers, and even some industry (TIC Council) have opposed this proposal from the start [[raw/papers/BEUC-X-2026-007-Digital-Omnibus-AI.pdf]]
- **Transparency gap impact:** Without clear labelling, consumers cannot properly assess online information reliability, request explanations, or lodge complaints — severely limiting enforcement of prohibitions on unacceptable AI practices [[raw/papers/BEUC-X-2026-007-Digital-Omnibus-AI.pdf]]
- **Generative AI disclosure gap:** Suspension of labelling for AI-generated/manipulated content leaves consumers unable to assess reliability — especially concerning in advertising, influencer marketing, and disinformation [[raw/papers/BEUC-X-2026-007-Digital-Omnibus-AI.pdf]]
