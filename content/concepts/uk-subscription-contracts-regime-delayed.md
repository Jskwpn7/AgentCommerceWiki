# UK Consumer Law: Subscription Contracts Regime Delayed

**Current status:** Delayed to early 2027
**Previously expected:** Spring/Autumn 2026
**Legislation:** Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 (DMCCA)

## Overview

The subscription contracts regime under the DMCCA, originally expected to commence in spring or autumn 2026, has been delayed by the Department for Business and Trade (DBT) to early 2027. The DBT consultation response published on 2 April 2026 confirmed this new timeline.

## Regime Content

The subscription contracts rules cover:

- **Pre-contract information:** Enhanced disclosure requirements before consumers enter subscription agreements
- **Renewal reminders:** Mandatory reminders before automatic renewals
- **Cancellation rights:** Simplified cancellation procedures
- **Free-trial transitions:** Rules governing transitions from free trials to paid subscriptions

## Enforcement Gap

While the DMCCA subscription regime is delayed, enforcement under existing unfair commercial practices provisions continues aggressively. The CMA is already acting against:

- Fake reviews (Autotrader/Feefo/Dignity/Just Eat/Pasta Evangelists)
- Drip pricing (Ryanair)
- Misleading subscription marketing (Microsoft Copilot investigation, opened 27 July 2026)

See also: [[CMA Microsoft Subscription Investigation (Copilot)]]

## Compliance Implications

- **Businesses:** Additional preparation time for DMCCA compliance
- **Consumers:** No immediate change to existing protections
- **Regulators:** CMA continues enforcement using existing powers
