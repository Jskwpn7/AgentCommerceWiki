# AI Office — European Commission AI Office

## Overview

The AI Office is a body within the European Commission established to coordinate and oversee implementation of the EU AI Act. It handles coordination of national authorities, contributes to harmonised standards development, and exercises oversight over general-purpose AI models.

## Key Dates and Facts

- **Status (as of Feb 2026):** In the process of becoming operational
- **Current challenges:** Already facing difficulties in fulfilling existing coordination and oversight tasks with allocated resources
- **Proposed new powers (Digital Omnibus):** Direct supervisory and enforcement powers over general-purpose AI-based systems and AI systems integrated into VLOPs/VLOSEs under the Digital Services Act

## Documented Harms or Failures

- **Resourcing gap:** Adding direct supervisory and enforcement powers over complex AI systems requires significant additional expertise, staffing, and financial resources — currently not addressed [[ai-office-centralisation]]
- **Enforcement bottleneck risk:** Centralisation without adequate resourcing risks making enforcement slower and less effective, creating bottlenecks at EU level and weakening consumer protection [[ai-office-centralisation]]
- **Gatekeeper risk:** Proposed Article 77 changes could require fundamental rights authorities to go through market surveillance authorities as intermediaries, creating procedural barriers to enforcement [[ai-office-centralisation]]
