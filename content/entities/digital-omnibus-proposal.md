# Digital Omnibus on AI — EU Simplification Package

## Overview

The Digital Omnibus is a European Commission proposal to modify multiple pieces of EU digital legislation through a "simplification" package. It targets the AI Act among other digital laws, proposing changes that BEUC and other civil society groups argue go well beyond technical simplification and amount to a full reopening of the AI Act.

## Key Dates and Facts

- **Announced:** 2025 (referenced in EC Implementation Dialogue, July 2025)
- **Call for evidence:** September 2025 (Ares(2025)7724296)
- **Corporate Europe Observatory report:** January 2026 — "how Big Tech shaped the EU's roll-back of digital rights"
- **BEUC formal response:** 5 February 2026 (BEUC-X-2026-007)

## Documented Harms or Failures

- **Procedural bypass:** Proposed without observing key procedural safeguards, without clear evidence or impact assessment as required by Better Regulation principles [[digital-omnibus-proposal]]
- **Big Tech influence:** Corporate Europe Observatory reported that the proposal in detail seems to mostly respond to the demands of Big Tech [[digital-omnibus-proposal]]
- **Benefit distribution:** The companies standing to benefit most are dominant data industry players — "all but European" [[digital-omnibus-proposal]]
- **Self-declared deregulation:** Despite EC assurances that this is "targeted modification," BEUC characterises it as a reopening that diminishes existing rights and protections [[digital-omnibus-proposal]]
