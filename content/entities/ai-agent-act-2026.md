# AI AGENT Act

## Overview

The **AI AGENT Act** is a discussion draft of US federal legislation proposed by Senator Mark Warner (D-VA) on **June 29, 2026**. It is the first piece of legislation specifically targeting AI agents as a distinct regulatory category.

## Key Provisions

1. **FTC Registry of Trusted AI Agents** — Requires consumer-facing AI agents to register with the FTC
2. **Fiduciary-like Duties** — Applies to "custodial user agents" (those accessing email, e-commerce, credit cards, or sensitive personal data)
3. **Prohibition on Secondary Commercial Use** — Prevents AI agents from repurposing user data for commercial use
4. **NIST Authentication Standards** — Requires NIST to develop technical standards for authenticating AI agents

## Regulatory Significance

- First federal legislation creating fiduciary-like duties for AI agents
- Complements the FTC's July 1, 2026 "Suppression of Accuracy" policy statement
- Represents a shift from voluntary frameworks to potential legal mandates

## UK Policy Relevance

- Could inform UK regulatory approach to high-risk AI agents
- Fiduciary-duty concept aligns with UK's consumer protection principles
- NIST authentication framework could serve as reference for UK standards
