# Hiya "State of the Call 2026" Deepfake Report

> **Publisher:** Hiya (telecommunications security company)
> **Date:** March 2, 2026
> **URL:** [Press Release](https://www.hiya.com/newsroom/press-releases/state-of-the-call-2026-ai-deepfake-voice-calls-hit-1-in-4-americans-as-consumers-say-scammers-are-beating-mobile-network-operators-2-to-1)
> **Type:** Consumer survey + fraud analytics report

## Overview

A comprehensive survey of 12,000+ consumers across six countries (US, UK, Canada, France, Germany, Spain) documenting the rapid escalation of AI deepfake voice scams. The report reveals that deepfake fraud accounts for **6.5% of all fraud attempts globally** — up from 0.1% in 2022, representing a **2,137% increase** in just four years.

## Key Findings

### Consumer Exposure

| Country | Exposure Rate |
|---------|--------------|
| **United States** | 1 in 4 (25%) received deepfake voice calls in past 12 months |
| **United Kingdom** | 26% received deepfake voice calls |
| **Other countries** | Data collected across Canada, France, Germany, Spain |

### Fraud Escalation

- **Deepfake fraud share:** 6.5% of all fraud attempts globally (up from 0.1% in 2022)
- **Annual increase rate:** 2,137% growth in deepfake fraud over 4 years
- **Agentic AI enablement:** Fraudsters operating from Georgia used agentic AI to clone voices and video, contacting 600+ UK victims

### Consumer Impact

| Metric | Finding |
|--------|---------|
| Provider switching readiness | 38% of consumers ready to switch providers due to deepfake fraud |
| Trust in telecoms | Declining confidence in voice-based identity verification |
| Consumer awareness | Growing recognition that AI-generated content is pervasive (84% report encountering AI content regularly) |

### Pindrop Analysis (UK-specific)

- 26% of UK residents had received deepfake voice calls
- Agentic AI used to clone voices/video at scale
- Fraudsters contacted 600+ UK victims using cloned identities
- Voice-based identity verification (used by all major UK banks) faces **existential risk**

## Consumer Harm Vectors

| Harm Type | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Identity theft** | Voice cloning enables impersonation of trusted contacts and financial institutions |

| **Discriminatory targeting** | Voice cloning may disproportionately target demographics with less technical literacy |
| **Regulatory gap** | No existing framework addresses AI-generated voice fraud specifically |

## Regulatory Relevance

### UK Context

- **FCA Mills Review** specifically addresses "amplified financial crime" from AI, including voice-based fraud
- **Ofcom** has jurisdiction over telecoms but no specific deepfake voice regulation exists
- **All major UK banks** use voice-based identity verification, which faces systemic risk
- **38% consumer readiness to switch providers** represents massive churn risk for telecom and banking sectors
