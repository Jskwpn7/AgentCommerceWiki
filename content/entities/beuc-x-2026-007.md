# BEUC-X-2026-007 — Consultation Response

## Overview

BEUC's formal consultation response to the EU Digital Omnibus on AI, published 5 February 2026. The document provides a detailed, section-by-section analysis of how the proposed amendments to the AI Act would undermine consumer protections, and offers six specific recommendations.

## Key Dates and Facts

- **Published:** 5 February 2026
- **Document coordinator:** Cláudio Teixeira
- **Reference:** BEUC-X-2026-007
- **Type:** Response to consultation
- **Contact:** digital@beuc.eu
- **Length:** 14 pages

## Documented Harms or Failures

The report documents six major areas of consumer harm risk from the Digital Omnibus proposal:

1. Expansion of sensitive data processing for "bias detection" — [[bias-detection-data-collection]]
2. Deletion of registration obligations for self-exempted AI systems — [[ai-act-self-exemption-loophole]]
3. Extension of SME exemptions to mid-cap companies — [[sme-exemption-expansion]]
4. Reversal of AI literacy obligations — [[ai-literacy-reversal]]
5. Governance centralisation without adequate resourcing — [[ai-office-centralisation]]
6. "Stop-the-clock" delays to consumer protections — [[stop-the-clock-ai-act]]
