# Ryanair CMA Investigation — Mandatory Family Seating Fees

**Opened:** 2026-06-10
**Entity:** Ryanair Holdings plc
**Regulator:** Competition and Markets Authority (CMA)
**Source:** [CMA Case Page](https://www.gov.uk/cma-cases/ryanair)

## Summary

The CMA opened an investigation into Ryanair's practice of presenting mandatory family seating fees as optional. The airline requires families with children under 12 to sit together, but presents the cost of reserving adjacent seats as an optional add-on rather than a necessary component of the core fare.

## Consumer Harm Mechanism

This case extends DMCCA enforcement **beyond straightforward drip pricing** into the territory of **unfair contract terms** — where consumers are forced into a bundled charge because the airline's policy (mandatory family seating) makes the add-on effectively compulsory.

## Regulatory Significance

- Third significant DMCCA enforcement after AA (£4.2M) and StubHub (£900,000).
- Signals CMA is broadening its enforcement scope from hidden fees to **mandatory-but-presented-as-optional** pricing structures.
- Relevant precedent for other airlines and transport operators using mandatory add-ons disguised as optional upgrades.
