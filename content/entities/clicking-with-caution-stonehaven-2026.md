# Clicking with Caution: How Scam Adverts Are Damaging the Online Economy

> **Commissioned by:** Revolut and Stonehaven
> **Published by:** Public First
> **Date:** June 2026
> **URL:** [PDF Download](https://assets.nationbuilder.com/stonehaven/pages/5143/attachments/original/1782980463/PublicFirst_Revolut_Clicking_with_Caution_Report_3MB.pdf?1782980463)

## Overview

A comprehensive survey of 2,008 UK adults and 14 focus group participants examining the growth of scam advertising online, its impact on consumers and businesses, and public preferences for systemic protection measures.

## Key Findings

### Consumer Impact
- **51%** of UK adults see scam adverts "very often" or "fairly often"
- **1 in 4** UK adults have lost money after clicking a scam advert (≈13.5 million people)
- **65%** encounter scams most commonly on social media
- Average correct identification of scam adverts: **5 out of 8**

### AI's Role in Fraud Escalation
- **80%** think AI-generated scam content has increased in the past year
- **56%** have encountered scam adverts they believe used AI/deepfakes
- **84%** encounter AI-generated content regularly online
- AI makes scams "glossy, polished" and harder to spot

### Economic Impact
- **75%** trust online advertising less due to scam adverts
- **72%** more cautious about online spending
- **61%** have reduced online spending because of scams
- Smaller businesses disproportionately harmed

## Methodology

- Poll: 2,008 online UK adults (29 May – 1 June 2026)
- Weighted by age & gender, region, social grade
- 2 focus groups: 7 consumers + 7 business owners
- Advert identification test with genuine Revolut ads vs AI-generated fakes
- Public First member of BPC and Market Research Society

## Consumer Harm Vectors

| Harm Type | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Deceptive advertising | Scam adverts designed to mislead |
| Platform liability | Limited proactive removal of scam content |
| Financial harm | APP fraud losses; 13.5M adults affected |
| Trust erosion | Systemic loss of confidence in online commerce |
| AI-enabled fraud | Deepfake content at scale lowers fraud barriers |
| SME exclusion | Legitimate small businesses harmed by trust environment |

## Public Policy Preferences

1. **Faster removal** of identified scam adverts (49%)
2. **Stronger identity checks** for ad accounts (47%)
3. **Pre-live verification** of advertisers (47%)
4. **Financial penalties** for non-compliance (41%)
5. **Better reporting mechanisms** (40%)

Cross-party political consensus across Con, Lab, Lib Dem, Green, Reform, SNP for verification/enforcement measures.
