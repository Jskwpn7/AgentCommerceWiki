# The DMCC Act 2024 — Digital Markets, Competition and Consumer Bill

**Enacted:** 2024
**Regulator:** Competition and Markets Authority (CMA)
**Type:** UK legislation — consumer protection and competition law

## Overview

The Digital Markets, Competition and Consumer Act 2024 (DMCC Act) consolidates and extends the CMA's powers to address unfair trading practices, dark patterns, and anti-competitive conduct in consumer markets. Key provisions include:

- **Unfair trading practices** — Broader definition of unfair practices including manipulative design
- **Dark patterns** — Explicit coverage of interface design designed to mislead or manipulate
- **Binding consumer codes** — CMA can issue legally binding codes targeting specific practices
- **Penalties** — Fines up to 10% of global turnover for serious breaches
- **Enforcement** — Streamlined investigative and enforcement powers

## Consumer Protection Significance

Each DMCCA enforcement signals where the CMA is targeting pricing transparency:
- StubHub confirms focus on **hidden/additional fees** at checkout
- Marks Electrical confirms focus on **automatic opt-ins** for additional charges
- Ryanair investigation signals expansion into **mandatory-but-presented-as-optional** pricing structures (unfair contract terms)
