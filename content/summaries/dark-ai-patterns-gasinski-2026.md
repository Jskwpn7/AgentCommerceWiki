# The Impact of Dark AI Patterns on Consumer Purchase Decisions and Impulsive Buying

**Source:** European Review of Social Psychology (ERSJ) | Accepted: 25 February 2026
**Authors:** Huber Gąsiński, Barbara Szymoniuk, Magdalena Maciaszczyk, Maria Kocot, Janusz Soboń, Dominik Baldowski, Krzysztof Kandefer

## Abstract

Examines the relationship between consumers' susceptibility to AI-based manipulative mechanisms (Dark AI Patterns) and the level of impulsive buying behavior in an e-commerce environment. Two composite indices were constructed to measure susceptibility to Dark AI Patterns and impulsive buying behavior.

## Key Findings

- **Moderate susceptibility:** Results indicate a moderate level of both susceptibility to AI-based manipulation and impulsive buying
- **Statistically significant positive relationship:** Between Dark AI Pattern susceptibility and impulsive buying behavior
- **Empirical evidence:** Integrates research on impulsive buying with analysis of the manipulative potential of artificial intelligence
- **Composite indices:** Developed for measuring susceptibility to Dark AI Patterns

## Consumer Harm Relevance

- Directly relevant to agentic commerce — AI agents will be subject to the same dark patterns in their decision-making
- Empirical evidence that AI-driven dark patterns increase impulsive buying
- Composite indices developed for measuring susceptibility can be applied to evaluate agent-mediated consumer harm
