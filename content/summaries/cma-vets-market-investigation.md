# CMA Vets Market Investigation — Consultation on Draft Orders

## Overview

The CMA is consulting on draft substantive orders and undertakings for the veterinary services market. This affects pet owners' access to transparent pricing, switching, and quality information.

## Key Findings

- CMA consultation opened 3 August 2026
- Draft substantive orders and undertakings under consideration
- Focus on pet owners' access to transparent pricing, switching, and quality information
- No existing wiki coverage of vet services market

## Consumer Harm

Pet owners face information asymmetry, high switching costs, and emergency pricing in the veterinary services market. The investigation addresses these concerns through potential substantive orders and undertakings.
