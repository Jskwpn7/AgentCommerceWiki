## Overview

Existing virtual try-on methods lack customization — users cannot precisely control how garments are worn. Wear-Any-Way enables granular manipulation of try-on results.

## Key Contributions

1. **Customizable Try-On**: First framework supporting precise manipulation of wearing styles
2. **Sparse Correspondence Alignment**: Point-based control for specific garment locations
3. **Multi-Scenario Support**: Single garment, multiple garments, and model-to-model try-on
4. **User Interaction**: Drag-and-drop controls for sleeves, coats, tucks, etc.

## Technical Approach

- Strong pipeline for standard virtual try-on
- Point-based control system for precise garment manipulation
- Supports realistic scenarios with complicated backgrounds

## Implications for Consumer Protection

- **Misleading Representation**: AI-generated try-on images may not accurately reflect real garment fit
- **Manipulation Concerns**: Granular control could be used to make garments appear more flattering than they are
- **Expectation Mismatch**: Virtual try-on results may differ significantly from actual product
- **Transparency**: Consumers may not realize try-on images are AI-generated
- **Regulatory Gap**: Truth-in-advertising laws not designed for AI-generated product imagery
