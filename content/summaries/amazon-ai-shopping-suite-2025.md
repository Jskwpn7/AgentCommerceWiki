## Overview

Amazon's AI shopping suite combines personalized recommendation, agentic purchasing, and product discovery — all powered by generative AI and AWS infrastructure.

## Products

### Help Me Decide
- Appears on product detail pages after viewing similar items
- Analyzes browsing activity, searches, shopping history, preferences
- Recommends product with clear explanation of why it's right
- Shows upgrade pick and budget option alternatives
- Uses LLMs + Amazon Bedrock, OpenSearch, SageMaker

### Buy for Me
- Purchases from external brand websites when Amazon doesn't sell the item
- Agentic AI completes checkout on brand's website
- Customer receives confirmation from brand store
- Brand manages delivery, returns, customer service

### Things to Buy
- AI-powered shopping discovery feature
- Works alongside Interests feature for personalized recommendations

## Consumer Protection Implications

- **Opaque Recommendation**: Help Me Decide may prioritize Amazon's inventory or higher-margin products
- **Cross-Retail Data Collection**: Buy for Me collects purchase data from external retailers
- **Agentic Risk**: AI making purchasing decisions on behalf of consumers
- **Liability Gaps**: Unclear responsibility when AI recommendations cause harm
- **Market Power**: Amazon's dual role as retailer and cross-retail agent creates conflicts of interest
- **Transparency**: Consumers unaware of algorithmic factors driving "one-tap" recommendations
