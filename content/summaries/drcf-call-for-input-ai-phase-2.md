# DRCF Call for Input — Consumer Interest and AI (Phase 2)

## Overview

Phase 2 of the DRCF call for input (questions 17–28, tools and frameworks for consumer protection) closes 2 September 2026. This gathers evidence on what consumer protection tools consumers want for AI — a critical input into how the four regulators (CMA, FCA, ICO, Ofcom) will enforce existing law against AI consumer harm.

## Key Findings

- Phase 2 closes 2 September 2026
- Focus on tools and frameworks for consumer protection
- Questions 17–28 cover practical consumer protection tools
- Critical input into how CMA, FCA, ICO, and Ofcom will enforce existing law against AI consumer harm
- Responses may be disclosed under FOI 2000
- Updates the earlier review's Phase 1 reference (Phase 1 closed 3 July 2026)
