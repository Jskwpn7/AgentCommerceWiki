# CMA Microsoft Subscription Investigation (Copilot)

## Overview

The CMA opened an investigation on 27 July 2026 against Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd examining whether Microsoft 365 Personal and Family subscribers received clear information when Copilot was added to plans and prices rose.

Microsoft initially offered Copilot at no extra cost; later renewals came at higher prices. Pre-renewal communications are under scrutiny.

## Key Findings

- Investigation opened 27 July 2026 against Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd
- Focus on whether subscribers received clear information about Copilot integration and price increases
- Microsoft initially offered Copilot at no extra cost; later renewals at higher prices
- Pre-renewal communications under scrutiny

## Cross-Border Enforcement

- Australia's ACCC commenced Federal Court proceedings on 27 October 2025 (2.7M affected subscribers)
- Italian Competition Authority opened investigation on 26 June 2026 into whether conduct amounted to an aggressive practice

## Consumer Harm

The investigation centres on [[dark-patterns-choice-architecture]] and [[the-dmcca]] subscription contract rules. The pattern mirrors the DMCCA's concerns about pre-contract information, renewal reminders, and transparent pricing — but the CMA is applying existing consumer protection law (not DMCCA rules) before those rules take effect in early 2027.

## Timeline

Evidence-gathering phase through end of 2026.
