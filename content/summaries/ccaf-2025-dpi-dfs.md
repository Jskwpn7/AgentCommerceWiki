# DPI and Digital Financial Services: Convergence, Landscape and Regulatory Considerations

**Source**: [[ccaf-2025-dpi-dfs|Raw source]] • **Authors**: Avramovic, Juneja, Wu, Suresh, Zhang (CCAF, Cambridge Judge Business School) • **Published**: July 2025, with FCDO support

## Overview
Landmark study examining global trends for Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) through a policy and regulatory lens. DPI systems — digital identity, real-time payments, consent-based data sharing — are reshaping financial services design, delivery, and regulation.

## Key Contributions
- **Exploratory taxonomy** of DPI for digital financial services
- **Global landscape and case studies** across diverse national and regional experiences
- **Emerging technologies and regulatory challenges**: distributed ledgers, tokenisation, AI, digital wallets
- **Five DPI principles for financial regulatory authorities**
- **Cross-regulatory governance** of DPI, addressing fragmented oversight risks

## Core Findings
- DPI deployment can outpace regulatory understanding, creating knowledge gaps
- Multiple regulators involved with limited clarity on roles and accountability
- Jurisdictions with advanced DPI systems report better outcomes on financial inclusion indicators
- Fragmented oversight risks undermining both innovation and consumer protection
- Need for stronger cross-regulatory and cross-border collaboration

## Relevance
Digital public infrastructure, financial regulatory governance, cross-regulatory coordination, DPI taxonomy, financial inclusion policy
