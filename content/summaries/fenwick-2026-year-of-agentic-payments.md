# Is 2026 the Year of Agentic Payments? (Fenwick & West)

**Authors:** Andrew Klungness, Julia Arruda Rosenthal, Andrea Louie, Igor Voloshin
**Source:** Fenwick & West, 2026
**URL:** https://www.fenwick.com/insights/publications/is-2026-the-year-of-agentic-payments

## Summary

Fenwick & West's comprehensive analysis of the agentic payments landscape, identifying three key regulatory challenges and the protocol race underway.

## Key Points

### The Three Legal Challenges

1. **AI Laws & Regulations** — Layered framework (AI-specific + financial services + general-purpose laws); federal vs state uncertainty; Trump admin's March 2026 National Policy Framework calls for unified federal framework
2. **Money Transmission Licenses** — Platform control over payment flows is the trigger — platforms that can override, pause, redirect, or pool funds likely trigger MSB licensing requirements
3. **Consumer Protection (EFTA/Reg E)** — "Authorized vs unauthorized" transfer distinction doesn't map to AI agents; granting agent access to payment credentials is legally untested

### Protocol Landscape

- **AP2** (Google-led) — cryptographically signed mandates capturing user intent
- **x402** (Coinbase) — crypto-native A2A extension
- **MPP** (Stripe/Tempo) — machine payments protocol
- **Mastercard/Visa** — adopting agentic payments on existing infrastructure

### Crypto as AI-Native Currency

Stablecoins offer push-based settlement, 24/7 availability, microtransactions, and code-layered spending limits — advantages that make them natural partners for agentic payments.

### Liability Gap

"Who pays when the agents fail?" — user, developer, platform, or merchant? No clear answer exists under current law. Code-based authorization trails (AP2 mandates) are untested in courts.

## UK Consumer Harm Relevance

- **Authorization gap:** EFTA's "authorized vs unauthorized" framework has no UK equivalent that accounts for AI agents making purchase decisions on consumer behalf
- **Liability allocation:** The core question — who is liable when an AI agent makes a harmful or fraudulent purchase? — is central to UK consumer protection
- **Platform gatekeeping:** The platform-control vs pure-conduit distinction could inform how CMA/FCA regulate fintech platforms offering agentic payment features
- **Consent erosion:** Code-based authorization may provide weaker consumer protections than current UK consumer law requires
