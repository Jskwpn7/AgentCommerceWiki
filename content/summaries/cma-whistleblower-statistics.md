# CMA Whistleblower Statistics

## Overview

The CMA publishes statistics on whistleblowers who report competition/consumer protection breaches. This is a key data point on detection rates and the effectiveness of the CMA's leniency policy.

## Key Findings

- CMA publishes dedicated whistleblower statistics page (6 August 2026)
- Key data point on detection rates and leniency policy effectiveness
- Includes £250,000 reward for algorithmic collusion tips
- New dedicated page on GOV.UK

## Consumer Harm Context

Whistleblower statistics inform the effectiveness of the CMA's enforcement regime, including the £250,000 leniency reward for algorithmic collusion tips. Higher detection rates correlate with stronger enforcement deterrence.
