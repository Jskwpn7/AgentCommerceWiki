# FCA Financial Services AI Adoption Plan

## Overview

The **Financial Services AI Adoption Plan** was published by HM Treasury on **July 14, 2026**, developed by government AI Champions Harriet Rees (Group CIO, Starling Bank) and Dr Rohit Dhawan (Head of AI and Advanced Analytics, Lloyds Banking Group). The plan provides a framework for accelerating safe AI adoption across the UK financial services sector.

## Key Warning: Unregulated Agent Advice

The plan explicitly warns that **AI tools can produce outputs resembling personalised advice without meeting suitability, explainability, or accountability standards**. Where outcomes are poor:
- No consumer protection exists
- No recourse or redress mechanisms are available
- This creates a **material risk of harm** across savings, investments, mortgages, and retirement planning

## Priority Action

The plan's priority action is to **"Adopt a consistent consumer disclosure for AI-driven services"** — acknowledging that consumers cannot distinguish between genuine personalised advice and AI-generated output that mimics it.

## Consumer Harm Mapping

| Harm Type | Outcome Affected | Risk Level |
|-----------|-----------------|------------|
| Information Asymmetry + Unregulated Advice | Safety + Choice | HIGH |

The core harm vector: AI agents providing quasi-financial advice without regulatory oversight, with no consumer protection when outcomes are harmful. This is directly relevant to [[ai-shopping-agents]] as agent-mediated financial services expand beyond retail shopping into wealth management and insurance.
