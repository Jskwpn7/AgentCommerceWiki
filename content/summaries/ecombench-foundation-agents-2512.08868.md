# EcomBench: Towards Holistic Evaluation of Foundation Agents in E-commerce

## Paper Details

- **arXiv ID:** 2512.08868
- **Authors:** Rui Min, Zile Qiao, Ze Xu, Jiawen Zhai, Wenyu Gao, Xuanzhong Chen, Haozhen Sun, Zhen Zhang, Xinyu Wang
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08868
- **Date:** December 2025

## Summary

EcomBench is a holistic E-commerce Benchmark designed to evaluate agent performance in realistic e-commerce environments. Built from genuine user demands embedded in leading global e-commerce ecosystems, curated and annotated by human experts. Covers multiple task categories with three difficulty levels evaluating:
1. Deep information retrieval
2. Multi-step reasoning
3. Cross-source knowledge integration

## Consumer Harm Implications

- **Evaluation gaps in agentic commerce:** Most existing benchmarks concentrate on academic settings or artificial scenarios, not real-world consumer impact.
- **Consumer protection:** Without proper benchmarks, it's difficult to assess whether agentic commerce systems are safe for consumer use.
- **Real-world vs. lab performance gap:** Agents performing well on benchmarks may fail in production due to dynamic market conditions and diverse user interactions.

## Key Findings

- EcomBench grounded in real e-commerce contexts provides rigorous and dynamic testbed.
- Human curation ensures clarity, accuracy, and domain relevance.
- Three difficulty levels evaluate progressively complex agent capabilities.
