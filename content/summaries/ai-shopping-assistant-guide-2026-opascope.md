# AI Shopping Assistant Guide 2026: Agentic Commerce Protocols (Opascope)

Opascope | Max Thilén | April 2026 | [Full guide](https://opascope.com/insights/ai-shopping-assistant-guide-2026-agentic-commerce-protocols)

## Summary

Most comprehensive technical comparison of the two competing agentic commerce protocols (ACP and UCP). Documents how entire shopping funnels now happen in conversations with zero brand visibility — "no click-through, no landing page."

## Protocol Competition

**ACP (OpenAI/Stripe):** Live since Sept 2025, Apache 2.0 open source spec at agenticcommerce.dev. Integrates with ChatGPT's 900M+ WAU via Stripe payments. US-only currently.

**UCP (Google/Coalition):** Announced Jan 2026, backed by 20+ partners including Walmart, Target, Shopify. Integrated into Google Search AI Mode and Gemini.

## Consumer Protection Implications
The "zero visibility" shopping funnel raises transparency concerns: consumers may not realize they're making purchases through agent-mediated channels, and brands cannot track which AI agents drive conversions. This feeds directly into CMA harm vectors around transparency gaps and loss of consumer awareness.
