# Agentic AI Commerce Retailer Risk (Sheppard Mullin)

Sheppard Mullin | July 2026 | [Full alert](https://www.sheppard.com/insights/blogs/agentic-ai-commerce-the-next-wave-of-online-shopping-and-retailer-risk)

## Summary

Legal analysis from a major law firm identifying the core authorization question in agentic commerce: **does consumer consent to their AI agent override retailer terms of service?** This was addressed by a court that issued an injunction against third-party agents accessing a major ecommerce platform — ruling that agent access occurred "without authorization of the site operator" even when the user gave permission.

## Risk Categories
- New fraud/scams via AI agents
- Unauthorized purchase liability
- ToS enforceability against agentic transactions
- Privacy leakage (site → third-party agent)
- Agent authentication challenges

## Relevance to Wiki
Provides legal precedent for the authorization/consent conflict that underpins CMA harm vector #5 (agentic collusion) and CERRE's disclosure rules analysis. Confirms this is not theoretical — courts are already ruling on agent access rights.
