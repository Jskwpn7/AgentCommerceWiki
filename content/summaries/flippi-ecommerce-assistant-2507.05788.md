## Overview

Flippi addresses the challenge of navigating vast e-commerce product landscapes through natural language dialogue, delivering personalized shopping experiences that surpass traditional search methods.

## Key Contributions

1. **End-to-End GenAI Assistant**: Integrated conversational assistant powered by LLMs and RAG
2. **Multi-Technique Architecture**: Combines query reformulation, intent detection, RAG, NER, and context reduction
3. **Offer Identification**: Detects and presents most attractive deals
4. **Comparative Analysis**: Contrasts product features, prices, and attributes
5. **Cross-Platform Adaptability**: Designed for integration across various e-commerce platforms

## Technical Components

- **Query Reformulation**: Improves user query understanding
- **Intent Detection**: Identifies user shopping goals
- **RAG**: Grounds responses in product catalog data
- **NER**: Extracts product attributes and entities
- **Context Reduction**: Manages dialogue context efficiently

## Implications for Consumer Protection

- **Recommendation Opacity**: Natural language interface may obscure recommendation algorithm mechanics
- **Deal Identification Bias**: System's definition of "attractive offers" may reflect platform interests over consumer interests
- **Personalization Risks**: Deep personalization may create information cocoons and limit discovery
- **Competitive Neutrality**: Cross-platform adaptability could enable multi-platform aggregation without consumer awareness
- **Informed Choice**: Conversational interface may make recommendations feel more authoritative than they are
