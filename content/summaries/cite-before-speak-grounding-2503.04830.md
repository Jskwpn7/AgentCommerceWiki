## Overview

Conversational Shopping Agents (CSA) help customers with online shopping but face two critical challenges: LLM hallucination and lack of knowledge source attribution, both of which undermine trust.

## Key Contributions

1. **Citation Generation**: Paradigm enabling CSAs to provide knowledge source attribution in responses
2. **Auto-Evaluation Metrics**: Holistic metrics for evaluating LLM grounding and attribution capabilities
3. **Multi-UX-Inference System**: Scalable deployment appending source citations while preserving UX features
4. **Large-Scale Validation**: A/B tests showing 3-10% improvement in customer engagement

## Key Findings

- Citation generation improves grounding performance by 13.83%
- Customer engagement increased by 3-10% depending on UX variations
- Citation generation substantially improves trustworthiness of CSA responses

## Implications for Consumer Protection

- **Misinformation Risk**: Without citations, CSAs can make unsupported claims about products
- **Trust Building**: Citation generation provides consumers with verifiable information
- **Transparency**: Source attribution enables consumers to verify LLM-generated claims
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Citation requirements may help meet consumer protection standards for product information accuracy
