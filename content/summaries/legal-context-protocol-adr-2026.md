# Legal Context Protocol (Legal Context Protocol / LCP)

## Overview

On **June 24, 2026**, the American Arbitration Association (AAA) and Integra Ledger, together with a coalition of industry leaders, launched the **Legal Context Protocol (LCP)** — an open standard for attaching legal context to agentic transactions. Published under Apache 2.0 license with governance designed for transfer to a neutral foundation.

Gartner projects that by 2028, **90% of B2B purchases will be intermediated by AI agents**, moving over $15 trillion through automated exchanges. LCP addresses the legal infrastructure gap identified in this shift.

## What LCP Does

LCP makes governing terms:
- **Discoverable** before a deal — parties can verify legal context prior to transaction execution
- **Provable** afterward — via cryptographic fingerprint bound to payment, creating an audit trail
- **Jurisdictionally anchored** — tied to the law the parties chose to govern the transaction

## Founding Contributors

The protocol coalition includes enterprise technology providers (Google, IBM, Circle, UiPath, Wayfair), blockchain networks (Stellar, Ava Labs, Cardano, Hedera, Mysten Labs, Aptos, Sei Labs), and identity providers (Crossmint, Pinata, Baselayer, Trinsic).

| Legal/Enforcement Gap | Accountability | HIGH |

The existence of LCP as a B2B-focused legal infrastructure layer highlights the asymmetry with consumer-oriented agentic commerce: no equivalent consumer-protection overlay exists. LCP is designed for enterprise-to-enterprise transactions, leaving consumer agents without comparable legal safeguards.
