# Agentic AI Framework for Smart Inventory Replenishment

## Paper Details

- **arXiv ID:** 2511.23366
- **Authors:** Toqeer Ali Syed, Salman Jan, Gohar Ali, Ali Akarma, Ahmad Ali, Qurat-ul-Ain Mastoi
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23366
- **Date:** November 2025

## Summary

Agentic AI model for inventory monitoring, supplier purchase initiation, and trending product detection. Applies demand forecasting, supplier selection optimization, multi-agent negotiation, and continuous learning. Tested on a middle-scale mart prototype with three conventional and artificial data tables, compared against base heuristics.

## Consumer Harm Implications

- **Inventory manipulation:** Agent-driven inventory decisions affect product availability and pricing.
- **Supply chain opacity:** Multi-agent negotiation between suppliers creates opaque supply chain dynamics.
- **Demand forecasting bias:** AI-driven demand forecasting can create feedback loops that distort actual consumer demand signals.
