# Agentic Commerce Regulation Gap (Data Innovation)

Center for Data Innovation | Eli Clemens | March 2026 | [Full article](https://datainnovation.org/2026/03/agentic-commerce-is-coming-but-regulation-meant-for-humans-will-slow-it-down/)

## Summary

US policy analysis identifying two major regulatory gaps specific to agentic commerce:

**Consumer protection gap:** Regulation E (electronic fund transfers) provides no dispute resolution framework for agent-mediated purchases. If an AI agent violates consumer instructions or buys a product with an error-priced listing, consumers may lose all dispute rights — the strictest consequence of any electronic transaction type.

**Enterprise compliance gap:** Sarbanes-Oxley Section 302 requires executives to certify internal controls; unclear whether AI agent operating parameters satisfy this requirement. AI agents produce logs fundamentally different from traditional approval records with no framework for evaluating model parameters as audit evidence.

## Consumer Protection Significance

Directly relevant to DMCCA 2024 enforcement: if Regulation E's dispute rights are lost in agentic commerce, consumers lose protections they have in every other electronic transaction. The CFPB should clarify that consumer-authorized agents retain error resolution rights.
