# AI Agent Payment Security Risk Landscape (FluxA)

## Overview

Comprehensive overview of the agentic payment infrastructure published by FluxA in 2026, covering wallets, settlement protocols, agent identity verification (KYA), and risk control mechanisms.

## Market Context

- **Visa** predicts millions of consumers will use AI agents for purchases by the 2026 holiday season
- **LexisNexis 2026 report**: Agentic traffic rose **450% in 2025**, introducing a third type of digital interaction — existing fraud systems cannot distinguish legitimate agents from malicious ones impersonating them

## Four Risk Vectors

| Risk Vector | Description | Consumer Impact |
|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Agent identity fraud | Impersonation of legitimate AI agent identities | Unauthorized purchases in consumer name |
| Compromised credentials | Stolen agent keys/API credentials | Full compromise of agent financial authority |
| Prompt injection | Malicious instructions override payment destinations/amounts | Silent redirection of funds |
| Unauthorized delegation | Spending authority compounds across multi-agent chains | No single oversight point controls total exposure |

## Consumer Harm Mapping

| Harm Type | Outcome Affected | Risk Level |
|-----------|-----------------|------------|
| Fraud + Identity | Safety + Choice | HIGH |

The 450% agentic traffic increase combined with fraud systems that cannot distinguish legitimate from malicious agents represents a structural consumer harm: the payment rails are accelerating faster than detection infrastructure can develop. Consumers delegating purchasing authority to agents face risks that existing consumer protection frameworks do not address.
