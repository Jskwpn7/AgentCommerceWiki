# CMA Aldi & Lidl Groceries Market Investigation

## Overview

The CMA is considering designating Aldi and Lidl under the Groceries Market Investigation (Controlled Land) Order 2010, which would subject the discounters to the same rules as major supermarkets (e.g., prohibiting exclusive leases on high streets).

## Key Findings

- CMA press release dated 7 August 2026
- Consideration of designation under the Groceries Market Investigation Controlled Land Order 2010
- Would subject Aldi and Lidl to the same rules as major supermarkets
- Could reshape UK grocery competition, affecting supermarket pricing strategies and consumer choice in towns where Aldi/Lidl compete directly with the Big 4

## Consumer Harm

This investigation addresses competition concerns in the UK grocery market. Exclusive leases on high streets can limit consumer choice and prevent smaller stores from entering the market. The investigation considers whether the controlled land framework should extend to discount grocers.
