# FAQ on agentic commerce (EMarketer)

EMarketer | 7 April 2026 | [Full article](https://www.emarketer.com/content/faq-on-agentic-commerce-how-brands-should-act-now-compete-ai-driven-landscape)

## Summary

Industry overview of agentic commerce as a defining trend of 2026. Identifies five major players: Amazon (Rufus Auto Buy), OpenAI (ChatGPT with third-party apps), Google (AI Mode using Shopping Graph's 50B+ product listings), Meta, and Perplexity.

## Key Players
- **Amazon:** Rufus with "Auto Buy" for target-price purchases
- **OpenAI:** ChatGPT enables purchase completion via third-party plugin apps
- **Google:** AI Mode uses 50 billion+ product listings; UCP protocol with 20+ global partners
- Perplexity's agentic shopping tool

## Consumer Protection Significance
Identifies the structural shift: AI agents act as intermediaries between consumers and retailers, potentially bypassing traditional marketing channels. This raises questions about algorithmic bias in agent recommendations and whether retailers have a duty to be discoverable by multiple agent types.
