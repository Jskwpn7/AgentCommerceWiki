## Overview

RT-2 demonstrates how vision-language models trained on Internet-scale data can be incorporated into end-to-end robotic control, enabling emergent capabilities from web knowledge transfer.

## Key Contributions

1. **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models**: Category of models expressing actions as text tokens alongside natural language
2. **Co-Fine-Tuning**: State-of-the-art VLMs fine-tuned on both robotic trajectory data and internet-scale tasks (VQA)
3. **Emergent Capabilities**:
   - Significantly improved generalization to novel objects
   - Ability to interpret commands not present in robot training data
   - Rudimentary reasoning in response to user commands
4. **Chain-of-Thought Reasoning**: Enables multi-stage semantic reasoning (e.g., identifying improvised tools)

## Evaluation

- 6,000 evaluation trials
- Demonstrates performant robotic policies
- Emergent capabilities from Internet-scale pretraining

## Relevance to E-Commerce

Listed in awesome-agentic-commerce under "Supply Chain, Warehousing & Embodied Commerce":
- Robotic systems in warehouses and fulfillment centers
- Physical embodiment of agentic commerce (picking, packing, sorting)
- VLA models enabling robots to handle novel objects and instructions

## Implications for Consumer Protection

- **Labor Impact**: Embodied AI agents in supply chains may displace warehouse workers
- **Safety**: Autonomous robots in shared human-robot environments raise safety concerns
- **Transparency**: Consumers unaware of robotic involvement in order fulfillment
- **Accountability**: When robotic systems fail, unclear liability for delays or damages
