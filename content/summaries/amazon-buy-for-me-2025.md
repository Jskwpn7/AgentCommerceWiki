## Overview

Amazon's 'Buy for Me' feature extends Amazon's commerce ecosystem by using agentic AI to complete purchases from external brand retailer websites when items aren't sold directly on Amazon.

## How It Works

1. Customer searches for branded item in Amazon Shopping app
2. Sees results from Amazon, third-party sellers, and external brand sites
3. Taps Buy for Me on eligible items → Amazon checkout page
4. Amazon's AI agent (Nova/Claude on Bedrock) completes purchase on brand's website using encrypted payment details
5. Customer receives order confirmation from brand store
6. Brand manages delivery, returns, and customer service

## Key Details

- Currently in beta with limited U.S. customers
- Runs on Amazon Bedrock (Amazon Nova, Anthropic Claude)
- Customer is in control; brands opt into participation
- Amazon cannot see customers' previous or separate orders from other brands' sites

## Consumer Protection Implications

Critical concerns:
- **Opaque Agency**: Amazon's AI agent makes purchasing decisions on behalf of consumers — unclear what safeguards prevent over-purchasing or unauthorized spending
- **Brand Choice**: Amazon controls which brands participate, potentially creating preferential treatment
- **Liability Gaps**: When the AI agent makes errors, unclear whether Amazon or the brand retailer is responsible
- **Data Collection**: Amazon collects purchase data from transactions it didn't fulfill
- **Consumer Control**: "Customer is in control" claim vs. reality of AI-mediated purchasing
- **Regulatory Scrutiny**: Agentic AI completing financial transactions may fall under new AI regulation frameworks
- **Market Power**: Amazon's position as both retailer and agent for other retailers creates inherent conflicts of interest
