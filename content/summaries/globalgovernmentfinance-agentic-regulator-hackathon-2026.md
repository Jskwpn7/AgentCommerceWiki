# Global Agentic Regulator Hackathon — AI-Enabled Regulation

The Global Agentic Regulator Hackathon, announced 8 July 2026 by the Cambridge Digital Innovation and Regulation Initiative (C:\>DIR), is a first-of-its-kind global initiative to build "practical, explainable and deployable" agentic AI prototypes for regulators and public authorities. Total prize pool: $100,000 (~£75,000).

## Key facts

- **Spearhead:** C:\>DIR, run by Financial Innovation for Impact (FII), an international not-for-profit.
- **Founding backers:** BIS Innovation Hub, Global Financial Innovation Network (GFIN), Digital Regulation Cooperation Forum (DRCF), plus 35+ organisations including Ant International, Google, SWIFT, Ethereum Foundation, Innovate Finance (UK fintech trade association), and the Cambridge Centre for Alternative Finance.
- **Six challenge fields:** (1) agentic financial advice / high-stakes AI advisory; (2) agentic payments, commerce and oversight; (3) decentralised market infrastructure, smart contracts and AI agents; (4) AI-driven fraud and scams; (5) know-your-agent, digital verification and digital public infrastructure; (6) market manipulation, agentic herding and stability.
- **Timeline:** concept notes closed 31 July 2026; finalists build prototypes in week of 1 September on synthetic datasets from UK-headquartered NayaOne; winners announced in Cambridge (UK) on 18 September 2026, then present at Singapore FinTech Festival in November.
- **GFIN chair Colin Payne (FCA head of innovation):** "The future of supervision is collaborative."

## Relevance to UK Consumer Harms

- The DRCF — the UK's four-regulator forum (CMA, FCA, ICO, Ofcom) — is a founding backer, signalling active UK interest in using agentic AI to *supervise* agentic AI.
- Challenge fields 2 and 4 (agentic payments/commerce oversight; AI-driven fraud and scams) map directly onto the consumer harm vectors tracked in this wiki — see [[agentic-commerce-protocols]] and [[drcf]].
- The framing that "the pace and autonomy of these systems [are] beginning to outstrip the tools regulators currently equipped to supervise them" is the core SupTech capacity gap — relevant to the FCA's own AI sandbox programme.
- "Know-your-agent" (field 5) is a direct analogue of consumer-facing agent transparency obligations — the mirror image of the duties proposed in the [[ai-agent-act-warner-2026]] and the [[ai-act]].
