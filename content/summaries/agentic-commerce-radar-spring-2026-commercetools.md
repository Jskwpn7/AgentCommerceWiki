# Agentic Commerce Radar: Spring 2026 Update (commercetools)

commercetools Blog | 22 April 2026 | [Full article](https://commercetools.com/blog/the-agentic-commerce-radar-key-market-shifts-insights)

## Summary

Industry tracking report documenting the gap between consumer trust in AI-assisted shopping and AI-executed purchasing. Key finding: **65% of US consumers trust AI to compare prices, but only 14% trust it to place orders** — a 51-percentage-point gap that represents the central consumer protection challenge for agentic commerce adoption.

## Consumer Trust Data

- 63% European shoppers use AI for comparing options (McKinsey)
- 62% Australian consumers open to AI purchase assistance (Stripe)
- Gen Z and millennials show highest delegation trust (29-30%)
- 79% identify accuracy as the most important factor in AI shopping

## Market Signals

- Google advancing UCP protocol; Sephora developing independent AI path
- OpenAI's Instant Checkout paused, confirming deployment friction challenges
- Machine-readable product catalogs becoming competitive entry requirement

## Relevance to Wiki

Quantifies the trust gap that CMA and CERRE identify as structural harm. Provides empirical data supporting the "loss of agency" and "agent as unfaithful servant" harm vectors.
