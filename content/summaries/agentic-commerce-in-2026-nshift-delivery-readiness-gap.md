# Agentic Readiness Gap: Delivery Infrastructure in Agentic Commerce (nShift)

nShift Blog | Johan Hellman | April 2026 | [Full article](https://nshift.com/blog/agentic-commerce-future-of-ecommerce)

## Summary

Operational perspective on the "agentic readiness gap" — the disconnect between brands optimizing for AI-powered discovery while their fulfilment operations remain human-designed. Key insight: **delivery stops being a post-purchase function and becomes a pre-purchase ranking signal**. When AI agents compare merchants, delivery speed/cost/reliability become selection criteria before transaction.

## Market Context

- McKinsey estimates $3-$5T global retail spend redirected to agentic commerce by 2030
- Gartner predicts AI agents intermediate $15T in B2B purchases by 2028
- Google UCP, Microsoft Copilot Checkout launched 2026; Shopify reports 15x YoY growth in AI-powered search orders

## Relevance to Wiki

Quantifies operational readiness requirements beyond consumer trust. Links protocol adoption (UCP/ACP) directly to fulfilment infrastructure investment — a key consideration for retailer compliance under UK consumer law.
