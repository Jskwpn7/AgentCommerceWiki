## Overview

Perplexity has evolved from a search engine into an AI-powered assistant platform with features enabling multi-step task automation (Computer mode), organized research (Spaces), and structured deliverables (Artifacts).

## Key Features

- **Computer Mode**: Hand off projects for automated execution with polished deliverables
- **Spaces**: Organized research collections
- **Artifacts**: Generated structured outputs
- **Workflows**: Customizable AI interaction patterns

## Consumer Protection Implications

- **Opacity**: AI search results may not disclose ranking methodology
- **Task Automation**: Computer mode executing multi-step tasks raises accountability questions
- **Data Collection**: Conversational AI collects extensive user preference data
- **Source Attribution**: Search results need clear provenance for consumer trust
