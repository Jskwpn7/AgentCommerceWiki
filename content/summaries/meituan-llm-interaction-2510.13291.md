# Higher Satisfaction, Lower Cost: A Technical Report on How LLMs Revolutionize Meituan's Intelligent Interaction Systems

## Paper Details

- **arXiv ID:** 2510.13291
- **Authors:** Xuxin Cheng, Ke Zeng, Zhiquan Cao, Linyi Dai, Wenxuan Gao, Fei Han, Ai Jian, Feng Hong, Wenxing Hu
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13291
- **Date:** October 2025

## Summary

WOWService is an intelligent interaction system deployed on Meituan App using LLMs and multi-agent architectures. It addresses challenges in cold-start data construction, multi-turn dialogue performance, business rule adaptation, single-LLM limitations, and open-domain evaluation.

Key metrics: USM 1 -27.53% improvement, USM 2 +25.51% improvement.

## Consumer Harm Implications

- **Scale of consumer impact:** Deployed on Meituan App — affects millions of daily users in food delivery, local services, and hospitality.
- **Automated customer service deception:** Multi-agent coordination in customer service can create opaque handoff patterns that confuse consumers about whether they're interacting with AI or humans.
- **Lack of human recourse:** When automated systems fail, consumers may have difficulty reaching human agents.
