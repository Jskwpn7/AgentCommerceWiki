# Consumer Demand for AI Shopping (Checkout.com)

Checkout.com | 9 June 2026 | [Full report](https://www.checkout.com/newsroom/consumer-demand-for-ai-shopping-is-forming-fast-but-trust-for-agentic-commerce-is-still-catching-up)

## Summary

Large-scale consumer research quantifying the gap between demand and trust for agentic commerce. Key metrics:
- 33% expect 10%+ of purchases to be AI-driven within a year; only 27% trust any organization to operate an AI shopping agent
- Non-negotiable controls: spending caps (30%), instant revocation (29%), easy cancellation (28%)
- Average acceptable per-purchase spend without approval: £177

## Consumer Protection Significance

The **57% brand-switching rate** when agents find better value represents a fundamental challenge to current retail models and raises competition concerns. The mismatch between merchant expectations (agentic commerce starting with financial products) and consumer willingness (groceries at 41%, financial services at 15%) suggests merchants may be over-optimizing for the wrong use cases.
