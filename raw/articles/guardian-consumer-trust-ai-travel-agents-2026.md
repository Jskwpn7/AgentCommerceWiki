## Article Summary

The Guardian reports on a rapid decline in consumer trust toward AI-powered travel recommendation systems, citing a new survey showing that 68% of UK consumers have encountered at least one travel recommendation they believe was influenced by undisclosed commercial relationships. The article highlights growing consumer awareness of commission-driven AI recommendations and calls for transparency requirements.

## Key Findings

- 68% of UK consumers report encountering AI travel recommendations they suspected were commission-biased
- Consumer trust in AI travel recommendations dropped 23 percentage points in the past year
- 41% of consumers say they would switch back to human travel agents if AI recommendations proved unreliable
- Several major UK travel platforms have faced consumer complaints about undisclosed affiliate relationships in AI recommendations
- Industry bodies are debating whether to introduce a "disclosure standard" for AI-generated travel recommendations
