# AIO and GEO: The New Discovery Layer in Agentic Commerce

As commerce shifts from human-led browsing to AI-mediated discovery, a new layer of optimization is emerging.

## Key Concepts
- **AIO (AI Optimization / AI Overview Optimization):** Optimizing content specifically to appear within AI-generated summaries (e.g., Google AI Overviews).
- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization):** The broader practice of ensuring brands are the "preferred" answer when an AI agent synthesizes a recommendation for a user.

## The Shift
The "winning" brands will no longer be those with the best SEO (Search Engine Optimization) for humans, but those that understand how consumer behavior unfolds inside AI discovery environments.

## Consumer Harm Potential
- **Algorithmic Steering:** AIO/GEO can be used to stealthily steer agents toward higher-margin products or "partner" brands, mirroring the issues found in [[wu-2026-ads-in-ai-chatbots]].
- **Opaque Influence:** Unlike a "Sponsored" tag in search results, AIO/GEO influence is embedded in the synthesized answer, making it a form of "banal deception."
- **Erasure of Competition:** Small merchants who cannot afford AIO/GEO tools may be effectively erased from the AI discovery layer.
