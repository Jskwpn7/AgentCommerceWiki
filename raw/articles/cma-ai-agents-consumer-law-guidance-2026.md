# CMA "Complying with Consumer Law When Using AI Agents" Guidance

**Source:** UK Competition and Markets Authority (CMA)
**Date:** March 9, 2026
**URL:** https://www.gov.uk/cma-guidance/complying-with-consumer-law-when-using-ai-agents
**Analysis:** https://compliancehub.wiki/uk-cma-agentic-ai-consumer-law-guidance-2026, https://www.taylorwessing.com/en/insights-and-events/insights/2026/03/aq-ai-agents-cma-publishes-guidance-on-complying-with-consumer-law

## Overview

The **first-ever regulatory guidance** on agentic AI and consumer law from a major consumer protection authority anywhere in the world. Grounded in existing UK law — the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 (DMCCA) — and backed by enforcement authority that includes fines of up to **10% of global annual turnover** for breaches.

## Core Principle

> **The use of AI does not change consumer law obligations.** The same rules apply whether a consumer interacts with a human or an AI agent, and businesses remain legally responsible for the actions and outputs of any AI systems they deploy.

**Responsibility sits with the business** — the business deploying the AI agent, not the company that designed or trained the underlying model.

## Key Requirements

### 1. Transparency
- Businesses must label AI agents so customers are not misled into thinking services are provided by real people
- If the fact a customer is dealing with AI rather than a person might affect decisions, **you should tell them**

### 2. Data Governance
- Businesses should consider what data their AI agents need
- Must ensure compliance with data protection law
- Must have appropriate processes for disclosure, testing, and monitoring

### 3. Oversight
- Appropriate and effective policies must be in place
- Staff must know what to do if a problem is identified
- Clear lines of accountability

### 4. Fair Treatment
- Consumer law requires businesses to treat customers fairly
- This applies regardless of whether the customer interacts with a person or AI agent

## Enforcement Powers

- **Fines up to 10% of global annual turnover** for breaches of DMCCA 2024
- CMA has direct enforcement powers under the DMCCA
- No need to wait for new legislation — existing consumer law applies
