# Legal Context Protocol (LCP) - American Arbitration Association

The American Arbitration Association (AAA) has introduced the **Legal Context Protocol** to provide a legal wrapper for autonomous AI agent payments and commercial transactions.

## Purpose
To close the legal gap where autonomous software agents make purchases, arrange services, and trigger payments without direct human oversight.

## Key Functions
- **Dispute Layer:** Provides a structured framework for resolving disputes arising from agent-led transactions.
- **Legal Context Attachment:** Attaches legal context (terms, authority, limits) to agent-led transactions to ensure they are legally binding and contestable.

## Relevance to Consumer Harms
Without a legal layer, autonomous purchases could lead to "unauthorized" transactions that are difficult to reverse, or agents agreeing to predatory terms that the human user never saw. LCP attempts to formalize the "agency" of the AI agent.
