# Agentix: Agentic Commerce Protocol & AI Payment Infrastructure

Agentix provides a low-code, plug-and-play layer that enables eCommerce platforms and ISVs to become "agentic commerce ready." 

## Key Features
- **Autonomous Checkout:** Enables AI agents to perform the checkout process without human intervention.
- **AI-Driven Payment Rails:** Infrastructure specifically designed for agent-to-merchant transactions.
- **Merchant Inventory Transformation:** Converts traditional merchant inventories into a "transaction-ready view" that agents can discover and transact with instantly.
- **Agentic Commerce Score:** A tool for merchants to see how visible their products are to autonomous agents.

## Definition
Agentix defines **Agentic Commerce** as a paradigm where autonomous AI agents act as the primary shoppers, moving beyond AI as a recommendation tool to AI as a transactional actor.

## Notes
Agentix focuses on the "merchant side" of the equation—making the web readable and transactable for agents. This introduces a new intermediary layer between the merchant and the agent, potentially creating new lock-in risks or "agent-tax" scenarios.
