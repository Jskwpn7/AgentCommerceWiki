# SME Exemption Expansion to Mid-Cap Companies

## Definition

The Digital Omnibus extends existing AI Act exemptions and simplification measures for SMEs to small mid-cap companies (SMCs). These changes are embedded across multiple provisions (Articles 11(1), 17(2), 63(1), 95(4), 96(1), and 99 AIA) and would apply even to providers of high-risk AI systems, including simplified technical documentation, proportionate quality management systems, and reduced penalties.

## How it Manifests in the UK

UK SMEs developing AI systems could face competitive disadvantage if EU mid-caps get reduced compliance obligations — they'd compete with better-resourced rivals that don't face the same standards. Alternatively, UK companies exporting to the EU might lobby for similar exemptions, creating a regulatory race to the bottom.

## Examples and Evidence

- **EU SMC definition:** Enterprises with 250–750 employees, annual turnover not exceeding €150 million — quite substantial organisations with sufficient resources for dedicated compliance teams [[raw/papers/BEUC-X-2026-007-Digital-Omnibus-AI.pdf]]
- **Risk-based logic broken:** The AI Act is designed as a risk-based product regulation where obligations reflect the potential impact of an AI system on health, safety, and fundamental rights. Linking obligations to company size rather than risk "weakens this logic" [[raw/papers/BEUC-X-2026-007-Digital-Omnibus-AI.pdf]]
- **SMCs already deploy high-risk AI:** Facts show mid-cap companies are "fully able to develop and deploy high-risk AI systems that already affect very large numbers of consumers" in creditworthiness, recruitment, biometric identification, and access to essential services [[raw/papers/BEUC-X-2026-007-Digital-Omnibus-AI.pdf]]
- **Reduced compliance incentives:** Lowering SMC compliance obligations and regulatory scrutiny risks lowering safety standards and increasing consumer harm [[raw/papers/BEUC-X-2026-007-Digital-Omnibus-AI.pdf]]
- **SME competitive disadvantage:** Applying reduced penalties to SMCs places genuine SMEs and start-ups at a disadvantage, competing with larger, better-resourced competitors [[raw/papers/BEUC-X-2026-007-Digital-Omnibus-AI.pdf]]
