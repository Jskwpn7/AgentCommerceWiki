## Overview

RAILS addresses a critical infrastructure gap in agentic commerce: the lack of neutral mechanisms for determining whether autonomous agents should complete their negotiated transactions.

## Key Contributions

1. **Verification-Native Clearing**: Clearing mechanism built around verification rather than post-hoc auditing
2. **On-Chain Settlement**: Transactions verified and settled on blockchain
3. **Agent Transaction Governance**: Framework for determining transaction validity between autonomous agents

## Mechanism

- Agents negotiate, purchase, deploy code, and move funds autonomously
- RAILS provides neutral verification layer before settlement
- On-chain verification ensures transparent audit trail

## Implications for Consumer Protection

- **Automated Transactions**: Agents completing transactions without human oversight raises consumer protection concerns
- **Dispute Resolution**: When agent transactions go wrong, traditional consumer protection mechanisms may not apply
- **Financial Risk**: Autonomous fund movement increases risk of unauthorized or erroneous transactions
- **Regulatory Gap**: Current financial regulations not designed for agent-to-agent commerce
